How it works

Three steps to a shippable roadmap

UXexpert.ai reviews your product across design, engineering, and growth at once — then hands you prioritized, code-native fixes. Here's the whole flow, start to finish.

01

Add your product

Drop in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files, paste code directly, add screenshots for a visual review, or point UXexpert at a live URL or GitHub repository. Add design systems or brand guidelines to tune enforcement.

02

Tune the review

Choose the audience you are building for, set custom rules, and load expertise skills that teach the reviewer your domain — from telco BSS collections consoles to patient portals. The audit re-weights itself around your users.

03

Ship the fixes

Every finding is scored by severity, effort, and impact, and arrives with a concrete fix — often a code snippet you can paste in as-is. Export the report and get to work.

What each audit covers

Every run applies three lenses at the same time — not three separate reports, but one prioritized roadmap where design, engineering, and growth trade-offs are weighed against each other.

Design

A principal UX review of usability, hierarchy, and accessibility mapped to WCAG.

Engineering

A full-stack code audit of quality, correctness, and performance signals.

Growth

A strategist's read on conversion, clarity, and the paths users actually take.

Choose your engine

The local engine runs entirely in your browser with roughly forty-five deterministic checks — nothing leaves your device, and there's no key required. The optional Claude AI engine adds deeper reasoning and reads screenshots, using your own Anthropic API key sent directly from your browser to Anthropic. There is no server in the middle: we never see your code or your key.

Either way, you can sharpen the review by uploading expertise skills — domain packs that make the reviewer an expert in your product's world instead of a generic checklist.

What you get back

A single prioritized report: each finding tagged by lens, ranked by severity and effort, and paired with a specific fix you can act on. Export it as Markdown today; PDF, Jira tickets, Figma comments, and shareable links are on the roadmap. Re-run after each iteration to watch your scores climb.

A human in the loop. UXexpert produces automated and AI-assisted suggestions, not professional advice or a guarantee of compliance. Treat every finding as a strong first draft — a person should review, validate, and edit it before acting.
