How it works

Three steps to a shippable roadmap

01

Add your product

Drop in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files, paste code directly, or point UXexpert at a live URL or public GitHub repository. Add design systems or brand guidelines to tune enforcement.

02

Tune the review

Choose the audience you are building for, set custom rules, and load expertise skills that teach the reviewer your domain — from telco BSS collections consoles to patient portals. The audit re-weights itself around your users.

03

Ship the fixes

Every finding is scored by severity, effort, and impact, and arrives with a concrete fix — often a code snippet you can paste in as-is. Export the report and get to work.

Features

Depth that generic checkers can't reach

True multi-role synthesis

Not three separate reports — one prioritized roadmap where design, engineering, and growth trade-offs are weighed against each other.

Code-native fixes

Every technical finding ships with a corrected snippet you can paste straight into your codebase.

Audience tuning

Auditing for Gen Z mobile users in emerging markets is a different job than auditing for enterprise buyers. Priorities re-weight to who you serve.

Expertise skills & custom rules

Upload skills that make the reviewer an expert in your domain and your users, plus rules like AAA contrast and minimal motion. The audit enforces them.

Growth playbook

A retention roadmap, A/B hypotheses, and conversion fixes grounded in what is actually on your page.

One-click exports

Markdown export today. PDF, Jira tickets, Figma comments, Notion pages, and shareable report links are on the roadmap.

Version comparison

Re-run after each iteration and compare scores. Automatic history tracking is on the roadmap.

Private by design

Local analysis never leaves your browser. AI reviews go directly from your browser to Anthropic with your own key — no middleman server.

API for teams
