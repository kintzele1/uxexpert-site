FAQ

Common questions

Is UXexpert free?
Your first audit is completely free — no credit card. It runs all three lenses (design, code, and conversion) and returns prioritized, pasteable fixes. To run more audits, Pro is $49/month or $490/year for unlimited audits.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free is one full audit so you can see the value on your own site. Pro removes the limit: unlimited audits, plus saved history and richer exports as they ship. Everything an audit checks is identical on both — Pro just lifts the cap.
Can I pay monthly or annually?
Both. Pro is $49/month, or $490/year — two months free versus paying monthly. Switch between them or cancel anytime.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Pro is billed through Stripe, and you can cancel or switch plans anytime from the Stripe billing portal, or by emailing hello@uxexpert.ai. No contracts.
Is my code private?
Yes. The audit runs in your browser — the code you paste, upload, or fetch never leaves your device. The optional Claude AI engine only sends data if you supply your own Anthropic API key, going directly from your browser to Anthropic, never through our servers. Your account and billing are handled separately and never touch your code. See the Privacy Policy.
What does an audit check?
Every audit covers design and usability, accessibility mapped to WCAG success criteria, engineering and code quality, performance signals, SEO fundamentals, and conversion. Each finding comes scored by severity, effort, and impact, with a concrete fix — often a code snippet you can paste in.
Can it audit a live URL or a GitHub repository?
Yes. Paste a live URL and UXexpert fetches the page for review, or point it at a public GitHub repository — a branch or folder link works too. A GitHub token unlocks private repositories. You can also drag in files or screenshots directly.
How is this different from Lighthouse or an accessibility checker?
Single-purpose tools check one dimension — Lighthouse for performance, axe for accessibility. UXexpert.ai combines a usability, engineering, and growth review in one report, adds AI reasoning that reads your code and screenshots in context, and lets you tune the audit to your specific users with expertise skills. It complements those tools rather than replacing them.
